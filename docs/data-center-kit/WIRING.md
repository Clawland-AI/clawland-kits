# Wiring Guide & Safety
## ⚠️ SAFETY WARNINGS
1. **Power Off:** Always disconnect the main power supply before touching internal wiring.
2. **Static Discharge:** Wear an anti-static wrist strap to prevent damage to sensitive sensors.
3. **Moisture:** Ensure the kit is completely dry before activation.
4. **Short Circuits:** Double-check all ground connections to prevent fire hazards.
